Lock-free data structures:
types of non-blocking data structures:

lock-free
wait-free
Wiki: lock-freedom allows individual threads to starve but guarantees system-wide throughput
cons: decrease overall performance, even though they reduce the time an individual thread spends waiting - cache ping pong, false sharing
