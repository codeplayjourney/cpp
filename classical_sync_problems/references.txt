## `std::lock_guard` vs `std::scoped_lock`

__`std::lock_guard`__ (C++11):

- Locks __one mutex__ at construction, unlocks at destruction
- Simple RAII wrapper for single mutex
- What I used in the current implementation

__`std::scoped_lock`__ (C++17):

- Can lock __multiple mutexes__ simultaneously using deadlock avoidance algorithm
- Prevents deadlock when acquiring multiple locks
- More powerful and safer for multi-mutex scenarios
